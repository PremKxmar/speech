# Speaker H — recording script

Read this out loud. Everything below is made up — read it as written, don't put
in your own details.

- **14 answers, 14 separate files.** Name them `01` to `14`.
- Press record, **wait one second**, then start speaking.
- Quiet room, phone's normal recorder, held about a hand away.
- Read it like you're talking to someone, not like a textbook. If a word feels
  wrong in your mouth, change it — but keep Tamil words Tamil and English words
  English. That's the part being measured.
- One take each. Don't re-record, don't edit, don't convert the files.
- Send as **Document** on WhatsApp (not a voice note), or Drive/email.

### 01

Enga veetla amma, appa, naan, apparam rendu thangachi iruppaanga. Appa meen
pidikka poivaaru, adhikaalaila kadal ku poi madhiyam dhaan thirumbi varuvaaru.
Amma pidicha meen a santhaila vithuduvaanga. En periya thangachi patthaam
vaguppu, chinnaval anjaam vaguppu. Naan dhaan veetla mootha pillai, so appa
illaadha podhu veedu paarthukkanum. Kadal pakkathula veedu, so raathiri alai
sathham kekkum, adhu pazhagiduchu.

### 02

Nethu kaalaila kanji saapten, enga veetla kaalaila kanji dhaan vaipaanga. Kooda
oru kaaram irundhuchu. Madhiyam sadham, meen kuzhambu, apparam meen varuval.
Enga veetla meen illaama pandhi nadakkaadhu. Saayangaalam thengai um vellam um
saapten. Raathiri moonu dosai saapten, kooda madhiya kuzhambu meedhi
irundhuchu, adhula thoichi saapten. Amma nallaa samaippaanga, veliya saapdrathu
enakku pidikkaadhu.

### 03

Naan adhikaalai anju mani ku ezhunthuduven. Appa adhukku munnadiye kadal ku
poitruppaaru. Naan kulichi, kanji saapttu, aaru mani ku kelambuven. Veetla
irundhu perunthu nilaiyam ku pathinanju nimisham nadakkanum. Anga irundhu
perunthu pidippen, adhula naappadhu nimisham aagum. Perunthula kootam adhigam,
ninnu dhaan poven. Ezhu mani ku ulla college la irupen.

### 04

Poana vaaram konjam selavu aachu. Mudhalla thangachi ku pusthagam vaangunen,
nooru ambadhu rupaa. Apparam perunthu ku vaarathuku nooru rupaa. Phone recharge
irunooru rupaa. Amma ku marundhu vaangunen, munnooru rupaa. Naan oru chappal
vaangunen, irunooru ambadhu rupaa. Mothama aayiram rupaa maadhiri aachu. Appa
meen vithu varra kaasu dhaan, adhanaala thevai illaadha selavu naan panradhilla.

### 05

En kaipesi la WhatsApp dhaan adhigam, veetla ellarukkum adhula dhaan seydhi
anuppuven. YouTube la padam paatu kekkuven. Appa kitta chinna kaipesi dhaan
irukku, avaru ku call panna mattum dhaan theriyum. Naan Google Pay use panren,
kadaila scan panniduven. Padam edukka camera use panren, thangachi photo edunga
nu kettukitte iruppa. Gmail la college seydhi varum.

### 06

Naan ezhu mani ku college varuven, konjam mundhiye dhaan varuven. Modhal theory
class, apparam rendu class thodarndhu irukkum. Idaiveli la nanbanga canteen ku
poduvaanga, naan veetla irundhu kondu vandhadha saapduven. Apparam lab, adhu
rendu mani neram. Pandhirandarai ku saapadu. Madhiyam session la thookam varum.
Free hour la library ku poi assignment mudippen, veetla current pona padikka
mudiyaadhu. Naalu mani ku mudiyum.

### 07

Enga oor la Aadi maasam kovil thiruvizha nadakkum, adhu dhaan periya vishayam.
Adhukku munnadi oru vaaram veedu suthama pannuvom, vaasal la kolam poduvom.
Thiruvizha anniku padagu ellam alangaram pannuvaanga. Amma paanaila pongal
vaipaanga, kadalukku padaippu vaipom. Raathiri muzhusum vilakku eriyum, kootam
adhigam irukkum. Sondhakkaaranga ellam veetuku varuvaanga, moonu naal veedu
niraya aalunga irupaanga.

### 08

Enakku Singapore pakkanum nu aasai. Enga oor la irundhu niraya per anga velai
paakka poirukkaanga, avanga vandhu solra podhu ketkurathe nallaa irukkum.
Apparam Dubai, en maama anga dhaan irukkaaru, avaru anga irukkura building ellam
photo anuppuvaaru. Kadaisiya Malaysia, adhu kooda pakkam dhaan. Naan mudhalla
kappal la oru vaati poganum nu aasai, vimaanam la illa.

### 09

Kadaisiya naan oru Tamil movie paathen, oor la irukkura theatre la. Kadhai kadal
pakkam nadakkura kadhai, adhanaala enakku konjam pidichadhu. Hero character
nallaa irundhuchu, aana villain weak. Songs rendu nallaa irundhuchu. Second half
la konjam neetti irundhuchu, naan naduvula kadigaram paathen. Nanbanga kooda
paathadhaala jaaliya irundhuchu. Theatre ku maasathuku oru vaati dhaan poven,
ticket kaasu adhigam.

### 10

*(Shorter — about 20 seconds.)*

Naan rendaayirathu naalu la piranthen. Enga oor la oru thattu idli ezhu rupaa,
adhula moonu idli varum. Innaiku naan adhikaalai anju mani ku ezhunthen.

### 11

Enakku udambu sari illena naan mudhalla yaarum kitta solla maaten, appa velaikku
poganum nu nenaippen. Amma mugam paathe kandupidichuduvaanga. Amma manjal paal
kaaichi kuduppaanga, illena vethalai vachu edhachu panni kuduppaanga. Kadal
kaathula jaladhosham adikkadi varum, adhukku naanga marundhu edukkurathilla.
Adhigama irundha dhaan oor la irukkura doctor kitta poven. Maathirai saapdrathu
enakku pidikkaadhu.

### 12

Naan amma kooda santhaikku poven, sanikizhamai kaalaila. Arai kilo, kaal kilo nu
dhaan vaanguvom. Meen naanga vaanguradhilla, appa kondu varuvaaru. Thakkali arai
kilo pathu rupaa, thengai onnu irubadhu rupaa. Vengayam arai kilo irubadhu rupaa.
Murungakkai, kathirikai, apparam keerai oru kattu anju rupaa. Mothama irunooru
rupaa ku vaaram muzhusum kaikari vandhurum. Amma nallaa vilai pesuvaanga.

### 13

Naan Kanyakumari pakkathula oru kadalora oor la irukken. Enga veetla irundhu
kadal rendu nimisham nadai dhooram. Suriyan udhikkurathum maraiyurathum onnaave
paakkalam nu solluvaanga, adhanaala veliyoor la irundhu niraya per varuvaanga.
Enga oor la mukkiyama meen pidikkura velai dhaan. Kaathu adhigam veesum,
kodaikaalathula kooda kuliraa irukkum. Oor la ellarukkum ellaraiyum theriyum.
