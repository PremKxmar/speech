# Speaker B — recording script

Read this out loud. Everything below is made up — read it as written, don't put
in your own details.

- **14 answers, 14 separate files.** Name them `01` to `14`.
- Press record, **wait one second**, then start speaking.
- Quiet room, phone's normal recorder, held about a hand away.
- Read it like you're talking to someone, not like a textbook. If a word feels
  wrong in your mouth, change it — but keep Tamil words Tamil and English words
  English. That's the part being measured.
- One take each. Don't re-record, don't edit, don't convert the files.
- Send as **Document** on WhatsApp (not a voice note), or Drive/email.

### 01

I live with my parents and my elder brother, here in Chennai. My mother is a
teacher, she takes maths for middle school kids. My father works in a private
company, something to do with accounts, I never fully understood it. My brother
is in his final year of engineering and he's completely busy with placements
right now, so we barely see him. It's just the four of us at home. Dinner is
the only time everyone is actually in the same room.

### 02

Yesterday morning I had idli and sambar, my mother made it. There was thengai
chutney also. For lunch I ate at the college canteen, just a normal meals —
sadham, rasam, one poriyal. It was okay, nothing great. In the evening I picked
up some bajji on the way back. At night my mother made chapati with a kuzhambu.
I usually eat very little at night, otherwise I can't stay awake to study.

### 03

I get up around seven thirty, which is late, I know. I get ready, skip breakfast
half the time, and leave by eight fifteen. I have a scooter, so I ride it
myself. It takes about twenty five minutes if the traffic is normal, and closer
to forty if it isn't. There's one signal on the way that takes forever. I park
near the back gate because the main parking fills up early. I'm usually in class
by nine.

### 04

Last week I spent a fair amount. I bought a pair of headphones for two thousand
two hundred, which was the big one. Then petrol, around four hundred for the
week. I ate out twice with friends, that came to about six hundred between food
and coffee. Phone recharge was two ninety nine. And I bought a couple of
notebooks and pens for the new semester, maybe a hundred and fifty. So roughly
three thousand six hundred overall.

### 05

WhatsApp is the one I use most, obviously — family group, class group, project
group, they never stop. Then Instagram, mostly for reels, I lose an hour a day
to it easily. YouTube for lecture videos and also for music. I use Spotify when
I'm riding. Google Pay for everything, I genuinely don't carry cash anymore.
Gmail because all the college circulars come there. And Notion, I started using
it this semester to organise my assignments.

### 06

I reach college around nine. First two hours are usually theory, back to back,
same room. Then there's a short break and we all go down to the canteen for tea.
After that it's either lab or a tutorial, lab runs two full hours. Lunch is at
half twelve. The afternoon sessions are the hard part, everyone is sleepy. If
there's a free hour I go to the library, mostly to sit somewhere quiet. College
ends at four.

### 07

Pongal is a big thing in our house even though we're in the city. The day before
is bhogi, we clear out all the old stuff and clean the whole veedu properly. On
Pongal morning my mother puts a big kolam in front of the house. Then we make
sakkarai pongal and venn pongal in a paanai, outside if we can manage it. When
the paal boils over everyone shouts together. We offer it first, then eat.
Relatives come by through the day.

### 08

I'd really like to see South Korea. Partly the food, partly the cities, and I've
been watching a lot of Korean content so I'm curious what it's actually like.
Iceland is the other one, purely for the landscape — the photos look unreal, the
glaciers and the black beaches. And Dubai, but that's more of a realistic one
because it's close and cheap and half my relatives have been there already. I'd
want to travel by myself at least once.

### 09

The last thing I watched was an English series, I finished it over two weekends.
The writing was genuinely good, especially the first four episodes. The pacing
dropped in the middle, which happens with almost every show now. The lead actor
carried it though, there's one scene near the end that was properly acted. The
ending was a bit rushed, they clearly wanted a second season. I'd recommend it,
but with that warning.

### 10

*(Shorter — about 20 seconds.)*

I was born in two thousand and three. Near my place one plate of idli costs
fifteen rupees, and you get three idlis. Today I woke up at seven thirty, which
is normal for me.

### 11

When I fall sick the first thing I do is tell my mother, and she immediately
decides it's because I skipped breakfast. She'll make something light and tell
me to rest. If it's just a cold I take a tablet and carry on, I don't usually
take leave. If it goes past two or three days then we go to the clinic near our
place, the doctor there has been seeing us for years. I'm bad about finishing
the course of tablets though.

### 12

We do most of the vegetable shopping at a market on Sunday morning. We buy in
half kilos mostly, sometimes a quarter. Thakkali is around twenty rupees for
half a kilo. Vengayam is the expensive one, close to fifty a kilo now. Then
kathirikai, murungakkai, and one bundle of keerai for ten. My mother argues over
the price every single week and I stand there holding the bags. It comes to
about three fifty for the whole week.

### 13

I've lived in Chennai my whole life, so this is my oor even though my family
isn't originally from here. We're in a part of the city that's mostly flats now,
but there's still an old kovil at the end of our street that's been there
forever. The area is known for its food more than anything, there's a row of
shops that stay open till one in the morning. It gets loud, but I like that. I'd
find a quiet town boring.

### 14
