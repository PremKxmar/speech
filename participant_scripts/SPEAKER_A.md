# Speaker A — recording script

Read this out loud. Everything below is made up — read it as written, don't put
in your own details.

- **14 answers, 14 separate files.** Name them `01` to `14`.
- Press record, **wait one second**, then start speaking.
- Quiet room, phone's normal recorder, held about a hand away.
- Read it like you're talking to someone, not like a textbook. If a word feels
  wrong in your mouth, change it — but keep Tamil words Tamil and English words
  English. That's the part being measured.
- One take each. Don't re-record, don't edit, don't convert the files.
- Send as **Document** on WhatsApp (not a voice note), or Drive/email.

### 01

Naan enga family kooda dhaan iruken. Enga veetla amma, appa, apparam en
thangachi. Appa oru small business nadathuraru, hardware shop maadhiri. Amma
veetla dhaan iruppanga, morning full time samayal, veetu velai ellam paapanga.
En thangachi ippo tenth padikkura, romba nallaa padippa. Naan dhaan veetla
periyavan, so enna ellarum konjam adjust panni ketpanga. Evening la ellarum
sernthu dhaan saapduvom, adhu enga veetla rule maadhiri.

### 02

Nethu morning idli sambar saapten, amma dhaan panninanga. Thengai chutney kooda
irundhuchu. Afternoon college la dhaan saapten, canteen la meals vaangi saapten
— sadham, rasam, oru poriyal. Adhu appadi periya nallaa illa, but okay. Evening
la konjam bajji vaangi saapten. Night ku amma chapati panninanga, adhoda oru
kuzhambu. Naan usually night la konjam dhaan saapduven, adhikama saapta thookam
varadhu.

### 03

Naan morning six thirty ku ezhunthuduven. Ready aagi, saapttu, seven fifteen ku
veetla irundhu kilambuven. Enga veetla irundhu bus stop ku fifteen minutes
nadakkanum. Anga irundhu 47B bus pidippen, adhu direct ah college varum. Bus la
konjam crowd ah dhaan irukkum, but enakku usually seat kidaikkum. Suma forty
minutes aagum. Apparam bus stop la erangi, konjam thooram nadandhu college gate
ku poven. Eight thirty ku ulla ulla irupen.

### 04

Poana vaaram naan konjam adhikama dhaan selavu panninen. Mudhalla oru shirt
vaangunen, adhu five hundred rupees. Apparam friends kooda vela poi movie
paathom, ticket one fifty. Snacks ku thani ah two hundred aachu. Bus pass ku
month ku three hundred kattren. Apparam phone recharge two thirty nine. Mothama
suma fifteen hundred maadhiri aayiduchu. Adhula pathi thevai illadha selavu
dhaan.

### 05

En phone la adhigama WhatsApp dhaan use panren. Family group irukku, college
group irukku, so full time adhula dhaan message paathukitte iruppen. Apparam
Instagram, adhu suma time pass ku, reels paapen. YouTube la konjam study videos
paapen, apparam songs kekkuven. Spotify um irukku but adhu occasionally dhaan.
Google Pay dhaan payment ku, kadaila kooda ippo cash kudukradhilla. Apparam
Gmail, college mail ellam adhula dhaan varum.

### 06

Morning eight thirty ku college varuven. First hour usually theory class,
apparam back to back rendu class irukkum. Break time la friends kooda canteen ku
poi tea kudippom. Apparam lab irundha lab ku poven, adhu rendu hour irukkum.
Twelve thirty ku lunch, canteen la dhaan. Afternoon la konjam classes bore
adikkum, athuku aprom library ku poi ukkanduruven. Four ku college mudiyum,
apparam ground la konjam neram irundhutu veetuku kilambuven.

### 07

Pongal enga veetla romba periya vishayam. Adhuku munnadi naal bhogi, appo
pazhaya porul ellam veliya podhuvom, veedu full clean pannuvom. Pongal anniku
kaalaila amma periya kolam poduvanga. Apparam veliya adupu vachi pongal
vaipanga — sakkarai pongal, venn pongal rendum. Paal ponga porada pathi ellarum
koovuvom. Suriyanuku padaichitu apparam dhaan saapduvom. Sondhakkaranga ellam
varuvanga, mothama oru nalla naal.

### 08

Enakku Japan pakkanum nu romba aasai. Adhoda technology, apparam anga irukkura
discipline, adhellam paakkanum. Cherry blossom season la pona nallaa irukkum nu
solraanga. Apparam Switzerland, adhu padathula dhaan paathurken, anga mountains
and snow ellam paakkanum. Innoru edam Singapore, adhu konjam pakkathula um
irukku, cost um konjam kammi. Naan enga ponalum mudhalla anga food try pannuven,
adhu dhaan enakku pidikkum.

### 09

Kadaisiya naan oru Tamil movie paathen, theatre la dhaan. Story konjam slow ah
start aachu, but second half la romba nallaa irundhuchu. Hero character ku oru
proper arc irundhuchu, adhu pidichadhu. Background score super, adhu dhaan movie
ku periya plus. Climax konjam predictable, naan already guess pannitten. But
overall nallaa irundhuchu, friends kooda paathadhala innum jaalia irundhuchu.
Oru vaati paakkalam, adhuku mela illa.

### 10

Naan two thousand and four la piranthen. Enga area la oru plate idli ten rupees,
adhula four idli varum. Innaiku naan morning six forty five ku ezhunthen. Konjam

### 11

Enakku udambu sari illena mudhalla amma kitta dhaan solluven. Amma udane
kashayam maadhiri edhachu panni kuduppanga, illena manjal paal. Konjam rest
edukka sollirvanga, college leave panniru nu sollirvanga. Rendu naal la sari
aagalena dhaan doctor kitta poven. Enga area la oru clinic irukku, anga dhaan
poven. Tablet vaangi saapduven, but naan tablet saapdradhuku konjam sombal
padhuven, so avoid panna dhaan paapen.

### 12

Naan amma kooda market ku poven, usually Sunday morning. Anga kaal kilo, arai
kilo nu dhaan vaanguvom. Thakkali arai kilo, adhu suma twenty rupees. Vengayam
oru kilo, forty rupees maadhiri. Kathirikai, murungakkai, apparam keerai oru
kattu — keerai ku ten rupees dhaan. Amma dhaan pesi vilai kuraippanga, naan suma
bag pudichitu nippen. Mothama three hundred rupees ku vaaram muzhusum kaaikari
vandhurum.

### 13

Naan Thanjavur pakkathula oru chinna oor la irundhu varen. Adhu periya oor illa,
but romba amaidhiya irukkum. Enga oor ku pakkathula periya kovil irukku, adhu
dhaan famous. Vayal ellam irukkum, nel dhaan adhigama vidhaippanga. Enga thatha
kitta konjam nilam irundhuchu. Oor la ellarukkum ellaraiyum theriyum, adhu dhaan
enakku pidikkum. Festival time la oor full ah kootam irukkum. Naan ippo city la
dhaan iruken, but leave kidaicha oor ku poiduven.
