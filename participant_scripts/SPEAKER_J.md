# Speaker J — recording script

Read this out loud. Everything below is made up — read it as written, don't put
in your own details.

- **14 answers, 14 separate files.** Name them `01` to `14`.
- Press record, **wait one second**, then start speaking.
- Quiet room, phone's normal recorder, held about a hand away.
- Read it like you're talking to someone, not like a textbook. If a word feels
  wrong in your mouth, change it — but keep Tamil words Tamil and English words
  English. That's the part being measured.
- One take each. Don't re-record, don't edit, don't convert the files.
- Send as **Document** on WhatsApp (not a voice note), or Drive/email.

### 01

Enga veetla aaru per. Amma, appa, en annan, rendu thangachi, apparam naan. Appa
oru powerloom la velai paakkaraaru, Erode la adhu dhaan periya velai. Amma
veetla irundhe thuni edukkura velai konjam paapanga. En annan appa kooda dhaan
velaikku poraan. Periya thangachi twelfth padikkura, chinnaval seventh. Veedu
chinnadhu, aana ellarum onnaave iruppom. Raathiri saapdum podhu dhaan ellarum
pesuvom.

### 02

Nethu kaalaila idli saapten, amma five idli pottu kuduthanga, kooda sambar.
Madhiyam sadham, kuzhambu, oru poriyal. Enga veetla madhiya saapadu appa
velaikku edutrupaaru, naanga veetla saapduvom. Saayangaalam kadalai sundal
vaangi saapten. Raathiri dosai, moonu dosai saapten kooda milagaai podi. Naan
raathiri konjam kuraivaa dhaan saapduven, adhikama saapta thookam varum.

### 03

Naan adhikaalai anji mukkaal ku ezhunthuduven. Kulichi, saapttu, aaru arai ku
veetla irundhu kelambuven. Veetla irundhu perunthu nilaiyam ku pathu nimisham
nadakkanum. Anga irundhu perunthu pidippen, adhu vazhi neeleyum nirthum, so
muppadhu nimisham aagum. Perunthula kootam adhigam, seat kidaikkurathe arudhi.
Ezhu arai ku ulla college la irupen. Maalai adhe perunthula dhaan thirumbuven.

### 04

Poana vaaram konjam selavu panninen. Mudhalla thangachi ku pusthagam vaangunen,
nooru rupaa. Perunthu ku vaarathuku nooru ambadhu rupaa. Phone recharge irunooru
rupaa. Nanbanga kooda tea kadai la ambadhu rupaa. Apparam oru pen drive
vaangunen, munnooru rupaa. Mothama ezhunooru rupaa maadhiri aachu. Appa
kudukkura kaasu dhaan, adhanaala naan kanakku paathu dhaan selavu panren.

### 05

En phone la WhatsApp dhaan adhigam use panren, veetla ellarum adhula dhaan
pesuvom. Apparam YouTube, adhula naan padam paatu um study videos um paapen.
Instagram konjam dhaan use panren. Google Pay vachi dhaan kaasu anuppuren, appa
ku kooda naan dhaan anuppuven. Gmail la college mail varum. Apparam oru photo
editor app, thangachi ku photo edit panni kudukka.

### 06

Naan ezhu arai ku college varuven. Mudhal rendu vaguppu theory class, adhu
munnadi room la. Apparam break, canteen ku poi tea kudippom. Aprom lab, adhu
rendu mani neram. Pandhirandarai ku lunch. Madhiyam vaguppu la thookam varum.
Free hour irundha library ku poi assignment mudippen. Thervu nerungura podhu
dhaan naan seriousa padippen, adhu enga class la ellarukkum onnu dhaan. Naalu
mani ku mudiyum.

### 07

Enga oor la Mariamman kovil thiruvizha dhaan periya vishayam, Aadi maasam la
nadakkum. Adhukku munnadi veedu suthama pannuvom, vaasal la kolam poduvom.
Thiruvizha anniku amma paanaila pongal vaipaanga, kovil la padaippu vaipom.
Raathiri muzhusum kooththu nadakkum, oor la ellarum kootama iruppom. Maavilakku
ettra amma ennai vaanga anuppuvaanga. Sondhakkaaranga ellam varuvaanga, veedu
niraya aalunga irupaanga.

### 08

Enakku Malaysia pakkanum nu aasai. Enga oor la irundhu niraya per anga velai
paakka poirukkaanga, en periappa kooda anga dhaan irukkaaru. Anga Tamil pesura
makkal niraya iruppaanga nu solraanga. Apparam Singapore, adhu suththama
irukkum, kattidam ellam periya periyaa irukkum. Kadaisiya Dubai. Naan mudhalla
velaikku dhaan veliya poganum nu nenaikkaren, sutha paakka illa.

### 09

Kadaisiya naan oru Tamil movie paathen, theatre la, second show. Story oru
village background la nadakkum, adhanaala enakku connect aachu. Hero nadippu
nallaa irundhuchu, aana kadhai konjam pazhaya kadhai maadhiri dhaan. Songs onnu
mattum dhaan nallaa irundhuchu. Second half la fight scene romba adhigam.
Nanbanga kooda paathen, so jaaliya irundhuchu. Theatre ku naan adhigam
poradhilla, ticket rate adhigam.

### 11

Enakku udambu sari illena mudhalla amma kitta dhaan solluven. Amma manjal paal
kaaichi kuduppaanga, illena kudineer kaaichi vaipaanga. Konjam padukka

solla maaten. Kaaichal adhigam aana dhaan amma ku theriyum, appo avanga dhaan
koottitu povaanga.

### 12

Naan amma kooda santhaikku poven, gnayiru kaalaila. Arai kilo, kaal kilo nu
dhaan vaanguvom, aana veetla aaru per irukkurathaala konjam adhigam vaanguvom.
Thakkali oru kilo, muppadhu rupaa. Vengayam oru kilo naappadhu ambadhu rupaa.
Kathirikai kaal kilo, murungakkai naalu, keerai rendu kattu pathu rupaa. Mothama
munnooru ambadhu rupaa aagum. Amma vilai pesi kuraippaanga, naan pai thooki
varen.

### 13

Naan Erode la dhaan piranthu valarnthen. Enga oor thuni ku famous, powerloom
kadai niraya irukkum, theru muzhusum machine sathham kekkum. Kaveri aaru

dhaan periyadhu. Kodaikaalathula romba veyilaa irukkum, veliya poga mudiyaadhu.
Oor la ellarukkum enga appa velai theriyum. Naan veliya padikka ponaalum oor ku
thirumbi varuven.
