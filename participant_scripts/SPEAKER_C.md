# Speaker C — recording script

Read this out loud. Everything below is made up — read it as written, don't put
in your own details.

- **14 answers, 14 separate files.** Name them `01` to `14`.
- Press record, **wait one second**, then start speaking.
- Quiet room, phone's normal recorder, held about a hand away.
- Read it like you're talking to someone, not like a textbook. If a word feels
  wrong in your mouth, change it — but keep Tamil words Tamil and English words
  English. That's the part being measured.
- One take each. Don't re-record, don't edit, don't convert the files.
- Send as **Document** on WhatsApp (not a voice note), or Drive/email.

### 01

Enga veetla naan, amma, appa, apparam en paati iruppanga. Appa vivasayam
paapparu, enga oor la konjam nilam irukku, adhula nel um vaazhai um
pottirukkom. Amma veetu velai ellam paapanga, paati um avanga kooda dhaan
iruppanga. Paati ku vayasu embadhu, aana innum thaniya nadandhu kovil ku
poiduvanga. Naan dhaan veetla kadaisi pillai. Raathiri saapdum podhu ellarum
sernthu dhaan saapduvom, appo paati pazhaya kadhai solluvanga.

### 02

Nethu kaalaila idli saapten, amma anju idli pottu kuduthanga, kooda thengai
chutney. Madhiyam sadham, rasam, oru poriyal, apparam appalam. Enga veetla
madhiyam pandhi rendu mani ku dhaan, appa vayal la irundhu vandha aprom. Saayangaalam
paati sundal panninanga, adhu naan romba pidichadhu. Raathiri moonu dosai
saapten, kooda kaara chutney. Naan raathiri konjam kuraivaa dhaan saapduven.

### 03

Naan kaalaila anji arai mani ku ezhunthuduven. Kulichi, saapttu, aaru arai ku
veetla irundhu kelambuven. Enga veetla irundhu main road ku pathu nimisham
nadakkanum. Anga irundhu share auto pidippen, adhu sandhu vazhiya poi perunthu
nilaiyam ku kondu poi vidum. Apparam perunthu, adhula muppadhu nimisham. Kootam
adhigam, nikka dhaan vendiyirukkum. Ezhu arai ku ulla college la irupen.

### 04

Poana vaaram naan konjam selavu panninen. Mudhalla oru sattai vaangunen, adhu
munnooru rupaa. Apparam nanbanga kooda veliya poi saapten, nooru rupaa aachu.
Perunthu ku vaarathuku ambadhu rupaa. Phone recharge ku irunooru rupaa
kattinen. Apparam veetuku kaikari vaangunen, embadhu rupaa. Mothama ezhunooru
rupaa maadhiri aayiduchu. Appa kudukkura kaasula dhaan sammalikkanum, adhanaala
kanakku paathu dhaan selavu panren.

### 05

En phone la adhigama WhatsApp dhaan use panren, family group um friends group um
irukku. Apparam Instagram, adhula reels dhaan adhigam paapen. YouTube la Tamil
channels paapen, samayal videos kooda paapen. Google Pay vachi dhaan ippo kaasu
anuppuren, kadaila kooda scan pannuven. Gmail um irukku, college mail adhula
dhaan varum. Apparam Truecaller, theriyaadha number vandha adhula check
panniduven.

### 06

Naan kaalaila ezhu arai ku college varuven. Mudhal rendu vaguppu theory, adhu
munnadi room la dhaan. Apparam break, canteen ku poi tea kudippen. Aprom lab
class, adhu rendu mani neram odum. Panrendu arai ku saapduvom. Madhiyam vaguppu
la thookam varum, adhu dhaan kastam. Free hour irundha library ku poi
ukkanduruven. Naalu mani ku college mudiyum.

### 07

Pongal enga oor la moonu naal kondaduvom. Munnadi naal bhogi, appo pazhaya
porul ellam veliya podhuvom, veedu full clean pannuvom. Pongal anniku kaalaila
amma vaasal la periya kolam poduvanga. Veliya adupu vachi paanaila pongal
vaipom. Paal ponga porumbodhu ellarum koovuvom. Suriyanuku padaichitu apparam
dhaan saapduvom. Maru naal maattu pongal, maadu ku maalai potu kuliyattuvom.
Sondhakkaaranga ellam varuvaanga.

### 08

Enakku Sri Lanka paakkanum nu aasai. Adhu pakkathula dhaan irukku, anga Tamil
pesuvaanga, anga irukkura kovil ellam paakkanum. Apparam Malaysia, en periappa
anga velai paarthaaru, avaru anga paththi niraya solluvaaru. Anga Tamil makkal
niraya iruppaanga nu sonnaaru. Kadaisiya Singapore, adhu suththama irukkum nu
solraanga. Naan enga ponaalum mudhalla anga kovil ku dhaan poven.

### 09

Naan ippo oru Tamil serial paathukitte iruken, amma paakkum podhu naanum kooda
ukkanduruven. Kadhai a romba izhukkuraanga, oru vishayam sollarathukku pathu
episode edukkuraanga. Aana adhula oru paatti character irukku, avanga nadippu
romba nallaa irukkum. Naan theatre ku poradhu kammi, veetla TV la dhaan
paapen. Kadaisiya paartha padam oru Tamil comedy padam, adhu nallaa irundhuchu.

### 10

*(Shorter — about 20 seconds.)*

Naan rendaayirathu naalu la piranthen. Enga area la oru plate idli ettu rupaa,
adhula moonu idli varum. Innaiku naan kaalaila anji arai mani ku ezhunthen.

### 11

Enakku udambu sari illena mudhalla amma kitta dhaan solluven. Amma manjal paal
kaaichi kuduppanga, illena kashayam. Paati kitta sonna avanga naattu marundhu
kuduppanga, adhu kasakkum aana velai seiyum. Rendu naal la kammi aagalena dhaan
clinic ku poven. Enga oor la oru doctor irukkaaru, avaru niraya varusham a anga
dhaan irukkaaru. Maathirai vaanguven, aana pakkuvama mudikka maaten.

### 12

Naan amma kooda santhaikku poven, gnayiru kaalaila. Anga arai kilo, kaal kilo
nu dhaan vaanguvom. Thakkali arai kilo, adhu pathinanju rupaa. Vengayam oru
kilo naappadhu rupaa. Kathirikai, murungakkai, apparam keerai rendu kattu, oru
kattu pathu rupaa. Amma nallaa pesi vilai kuraippanga, naan pai pudichitu
nippen. Mothama irunooru muppadhu rupaa ku vaaram muzhusum kaikari vandhurum.

### 13

Naan Madurai pakkathula oru oor la irundhu varen. Enga oor la periya kulam
irukku, adhu pakkathula dhaan kovil. Vayal ellam suthi irukkum, nel dhaan
adhigam vidhaippaanga. Enga oor la ellarukkum ellaraiyum theriyum, oruthar
veetla nadandhadhu maruthavar veetla theriyum. Chithirai maasam la kovil
thiruvizha nadakkum, appo oor full kootam. Naan ippo padikkurathukaaga veliya
iruken, aana leave kidaicha oor ku poiduven.
