# Speaker I — recording script

Read this out loud. Everything below is made up — read it as written, don't put
in your own details.

- **14 answers, 14 separate files.** Name them `01` to `14`.
- Press record, **wait one second**, then start speaking.
- Quiet room, phone's normal recorder, held about a hand away.
- Read it like you're talking to someone, not like a textbook. If a word feels
  wrong in your mouth, change it — but keep Tamil words Tamil and English words
  English. That's the part being measured.
- One take each. Don't re-record, don't edit, don't convert the files.
- Send as **Document** on WhatsApp (not a voice note), or Drive/email.

### 01

There are five of us at home — amma, my father, my thangachi, my paati and me.
Amma is a nurse at a private hospital and she does night shifts every alternate
week, so the house runs on a strange schedule. My father drives a lorry on the
Trichy–Chennai route, so he's away two or three nights a week. Paati is the one
who's actually always at home. My sister is in eleventh.

### 02

Yesterday morning I had idli and sambar. Paati made it, she still grinds the
maavu at home. There was thengai chutney. Lunch was at the canteen — sadham,
rasam, one poriyal, and a vadai I shouldn't have bought. In the evening I had
bajji near the bus stand. At night amma wasn't home, so I made chapati and
finished the kuzhambu that was left over. I eat less at night.

### 03

I get up at six twenty. I get ready, eat something quickly, and leave by seven
fifteen. I have a bike so I ride it myself, and it takes about twenty minutes.
But there's one stretch where the road is dug up, and if I get stuck there it's
forty. I park the vandi at the back of the college because the front is always
full. I'm in class by eight.

### 04

Last week I spent about two thousand eight hundred. Petrol was six hundred, I
fill it twice a week. I got the bike serviced and replaced a strap on the
helmet, that was eight hundred. Food outside, maybe five hundred. Phone recharge
two ninety nine. And I bought a gift for my sister's birthday, six hundred. My
father gives me petrol money separately, everything else I have to manage.

### 05

WhatsApp for everything, including my father, who sends forwards at five in the
morning. Instagram, mostly reels. YouTube for lectures and for bike repair
videos, which is a rabbit hole. Google Pay, I don't carry cash at all. Gmail for
college mail. Google Maps because the route keeps changing with the roadwork.
And a fitness app I opened twice and haven't touched since.

### 06

I reach college by eight. First two hours are theory, then a break where
everyone goes for tea. After that it's lab, two hours, and that's the part I
like. Lunch at half twelve. The afternoon classes are the hardest, I've fallen
asleep in one of them. If there's a free hour I go to the library, mostly for
the fan. College ends at four and I'm usually out by four fifteen.

### 07

Pongal is the big one at home, mostly because paati insists on it. The day
before is bhogi, we clear out the old things and clean the veedu properly. On
Pongal morning amma puts the kolam, or paati does if amma is on shift. We make
sakkarai pongal and venn pongal in a paanai in the backyard. When the paal boils
over everyone shouts. We do the padaippu, then eat. We go to the kovil in the
evening.

### 08

Japan is the first one — the trains, the cities, and I've been watching a lot of
Japanese content lately. Switzerland is the second, purely for the mountains,
the photos look fake. And Singapore, which is the realistic one, because it's
close and half the people I know have been. I'd want to do one of them properly,
three weeks, not a six day package tour with a group.

### 09

The last thing I watched was a web series, five episodes, and I finished it in
one night, which was a mistake. Kadhai nallaa irundhuchu — they didn't drag it,
which is rare now. The lead actor was good, especially in the last episode. The
background score was doing too much in places. There's one twist I saw coming
from episode two. Overall time pass. I'd watch a second season.

### 10

*(Shorter — about 20 seconds.)*

I was born in two thousand and four. Near my place one plate of idli costs
fourteen rupees, and you get three idlis. Today I woke up at six twenty, which
is my usual time.

### 11

When I get a kaaichal there's no sympathy at all in this house, only
instructions, because amma is a nurse. She checks the temperature, gives one
tablet and tells me to drink water. Paati still makes manjal paal on top of
that. If it's just jaladhosham I go to college anyway. If it goes past two days
amma brings the doctor's number herself. I finish the full course, because
there's no way not to here.

### 12

We buy vegetables on Sunday at the market near the bus stand. Mostly arai kilo,
sometimes one kilo if paati is cooking for guests. Tomatoes were twenty five for
half a kilo. Onions are around fifty five a kilo. Then brinjal, drumstick, and
oru kattu keerai for ten. It comes to about four hundred for the week. Paati
argues over every price and she's usually right about it.

### 13

I'm from Trichy, we live near the kovil side of the city. Our theru is narrow
with shops on both sides, so it's noisy from six in the morning. The rock fort
is fifteen minutes away and I've climbed it maybe four times, all of them when
relatives came. My family's oor is a village near Musiri and we go there for
functions. Trichy is hot, that's the one complaint everybody has.

### 14
