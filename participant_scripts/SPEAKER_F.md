# Speaker F — recording script

Read this out loud. Everything below is made up — read it as written, don't put
in your own details.

- **14 answers, 14 separate files.** Name them `01` to `14`.
- Press record, **wait one second**, then start speaking.
- Quiet room, phone's normal recorder, held about a hand away.
- Read it like you're talking to someone, not like a textbook. If a word feels
  wrong in your mouth, change it — but keep Tamil words Tamil and English words
  English. That's the part being measured.
- One take each. Don't re-record, don't edit, don't convert the files.
- Send as **Document** on WhatsApp (not a voice note), or Drive/email.

### 01

Enga veetla kootu kudumbam. Naan, amma, appa, en akka, apparam chithappa
kudumbam ellarum oree veetla dhaan iruppom. Chithappa ku oru provision kadai
irukku, appa um avaru kooda dhaan kadaila iruppaaru. Amma um chithi um sernthu
dhaan samayal paapanga. En akka padippu mudichitu veetla dhaan iruppa. Chithappa
ku rendu chinna pasanga, so veedu full sathama dhaan irukkum. Ellarum sernthu
saapdrathu dhaan enakku pidikkum.

### 02

Nethu kaalaila idli saapten, amma four idli pottu kuduthanga, kooda sambar um
chutney um. Madhiyam veetuke vandhu saapten, kadai pakkathula dhaan college.
Sadham, kuzhambu, oru poriyal, appalam. Saayangaalam kadaila irundhu vandhu
thayir sadham saapten. Raathiri chapati, amma three chapati kuduppanga. Naan
raathiri konjam kammiya dhaan saapduven.

### 03

Naan morning six fifteen ku ezhunthuduven. Kulichi, saapttu, seven thirty ku
kelambuven. Naan perunthu nilaiyam varaikkum nadandhu dhaan poven, adhu ten
minutes. Anga irundhu oru town perunthu pidippen, adhu direct ah college varum.
Perunthula usually seat kidaikkaadhu, ninnukitte dhaan poven. Suma thirty five
minutes aagum. Erangi konjam thooram nadandhu college gate ku poven. Eight
thirty ku ulla irupen.

### 04

Poana vaaram naan konjam selavu panninen. Oru sattai vaangunen, adhu five
hundred rupees. Apparam friends kooda tea kadai la, adhu suma nooru rupaa.
Perunthu pass ku month ku three hundred. Phone recharge irunooru rupaa. Apparam
appa ku marundhu vaangunen, naanooru rupaa. Mothama fifteen hundred maadhiri
aachu. Kadaila velai seiyra podhu appa konjam kaasu kuduppaaru, adhula dhaan
sammalippen.

### 05

En phone la WhatsApp dhaan adhigam use panren. Kadai ku order ellam ippo
WhatsApp la dhaan varum, so naan adha paathukiduven. Apparam YouTube, adhula
naan niraya paapen — samayal, cricket highlights, apparam repair videos.
Instagram konjam dhaan. Google Pay kadaila mukkiyam, ippo ellarum scan pannitu
dhaan povaanga. Gmail college mail ku. Apparam calculator, kadai kanakku ku adhu
dhaan.

### 06

Naan eight thirty ku college varuven. Mudhal rendu vaguppu paadam sollikoduppaanga,
adhu munnadi arai la dhaan. Apparam idaiveli, canteen ku poi tea kudippom. Aprom
seyalmurai vaguppu, adhu rendu mani neram odum. Twelve thirty ku saapadu.
Madhiyam vaguppu la thookam varum. Oru aasiriyar irukkaanga, avanga paadam
sollikodukkurathu enakku romba pidikkum. Thervu nerungura podhu nool nilaiyam la
ukkandhu padippen.

### 07

Enga veetla Deepavali dhaan periya kondattam. Adhukku munnadi vaaram ellarukkum
thuni edukka poiduvom, kootu kudumbam nu kadaila periya list. Deepavali anniku
kaalaila four o'clock ku ezhundhu ennai thechukittu kulippom. Apparam pudhu thuni
potukittu periyavanga kitta aasirvadham vaanguvom. Chithi legiyam pannuvaanga,
adha mudhalla saapdanum. Aprom vedi vetuvom. Madhiyam ellarum sernthu virundhu
saapduvom.

### 08

Enakku Australia pakkanum nu aasai. Anga cricket ground ellam paakkanum,
Melbourne stadium la oru match paakkanum nu periya aasai. Apparam Japan, adhoda
technology um anga irukkura suththam um paathu aachariyama irukkum. Bullet train
la oru vaati poganum. Kadaisiya Dubai, adhu pakkathula um irukku, enga oor la
niraya per anga velai paakkuraanga. Naan enga ponaalum mudhalla anga market
paakka dhaan poven.

### 09

Naan adhigama YouTube dhaan paapen, cinema ellam kammi. Kadaisiya oru cricket
highlights video paathen, oru mani neram maadhiri irundhuchu. Apparam oru
channel irukku, avanga village la samayal panni kaattuvaanga, adhu enakku romba
pidikkum. Theatre la kadaisiya paathadhu Deepavali release, oru comedy padam,
nallaa irundhuchu. Aana ippo veetla TV la dhaan adhigam paapen, theatre ku pora
neram illa.

### 11

### 12

Naan chithi kooda santhaikku poven, gnayiru kaalaila. Arai kilo, kaal kilo nu
vaanga maatom — kootu kudumbam nu rendu kilo, moonu kilo nu dhaan vaanguvom.
Thakkali rendu kilo, adhu eighty rupees. Vengayam moonu kilo, nooru ambadhu
rupaa. Kathirikai, murungakkai, keerai rendu kattu. Mothama thousand rupees
maadhiri aayidum. Chithi dhaan vilai pesuvaanga, naan pai a thooki varen.
